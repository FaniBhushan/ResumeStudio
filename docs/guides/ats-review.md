# Free AI-assisted ATS CV review

Resume Studio includes an optional **AI-assisted ATS alignment review** that compares your CV with a target job description. It is part of a free, open-source desktop CV builder; the AI provider is selected, installed, and authenticated separately.

The review can identify:

- Terms and requirements that appear in both documents
- Relevant job requirements that do not appear in the CV
- Evidence gaps to consider filling with truthful examples
- Formatting concerns visible from the CV content
- Specific rewrite suggestions tied to existing CV fields

The review does not modify your CV. You read the findings and choose whether to apply a suggestion.

## How to run a review

1. Open **CV Builder** and load or create a CV.
2. Install and sign in to [Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, or Claude Code](../getting-started/ai-providers.md).
3. Turn on **ATS mode** in the sidebar.
4. Add the job description.
5. Open the **ATS Checker** tab and select **Check ATS alignment**.
6. Review the findings. Apply only changes that accurately represent your experience.

## What “ATS checker” means here

This is a content-based comparison to help you tailor a CV. It does **not** connect to an employer's applicant tracking system, test your PDF in a specific ATS, produce a numeric score, or guarantee that an employer's software will accept or rank your application a certain way.

AI-generated suggestions can be wrong. Check every name, date, skill, and claim. Do not add a skill or result just because it appears in the job description.

## Privacy and provider use

The app runs locally and stores your files on your computer. When you run an AI review, Resume Studio sends the CV and job description text needed for that action to the provider CLI you selected. That provider handles the request under its own terms, account settings, and privacy policy. Provider charges or usage limits may apply. ATS mode is off by default.
