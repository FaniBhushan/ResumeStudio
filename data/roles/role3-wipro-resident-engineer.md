# Role: Senior Software Engineer - Resident Engineer (Panasonic Automotive Europe)
**Company:** Wipro Technologies  
**Dates:** 10/2013 - 08/2015  
**Location:** Stuttgart, Germany and Bangalore, India  

## Project Description
- Supported the integration, system validation, and defect triage for the NTG5.1 Mercedes-Benz automotive infotainment software platform.
- Served as Onsite Resident Engineer in Stuttgart, Germany for 18 months, interfacing between the customer (Panasonic Automotive Europe / Daimler), offshore development teams in India, and partners in the USA.

## Responsibilities & Achievements
- Managed technical communication and aligned stakeholder roadmaps between onsite client engineering and offshore development teams.
- Led defect management and triage for the domain, investigating system logs, analyzing root causes, and delegating fixes to developers.
- Participated in validation vehicle test drives with Mercedes-Benz engineers to verify release quality and diagnose software anomalies under real driving conditions.
- Spearheaded stakeholder management and negotiated defect resolution timelines during client alignment meetings.
- Prototyped a hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) mock communication gateway using a Wandboard Quad (ARM Debian Linux) to simulate connected services and test browser-based in-car applications.

## Technologies
- Debian Linux, Apache, PHP, Perl, JavaScript, HTML, Bash, IBM Rational Team Concert (RTC), Wandboard Quad (ARM)
