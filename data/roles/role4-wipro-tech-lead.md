# Role: Software Technical Lead - UI Testing Automation (Panasonic Japan Client)
**Company:** Wipro Technologies  
**Dates:** 10/2015 - 01/2018  
**Location:** Bangalore, India and Yokohama, Japan

- Defined engineering processes, QA standards, and automated testing guidelines, improving overall team efficiency and validation reliability.
