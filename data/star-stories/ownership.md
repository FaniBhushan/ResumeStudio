# STAR Story: Ownership

## Situation
*Describe the context, the environment, or the problem you faced.*

## Task
*What was the goal or the specific problem you needed to solve? What was your responsibility?*

## Action
*What actions did you take? Explain the how and why. Be specific about your contribution, technical choices, and leadership.*

## Result
*What was the outcome? Use quantitative metrics and qualitative indicators (e.g., delivered on time, reduced latency by X%, improved team velocity).*
