# STAR Story: Conflict Resolution

## Situation
*Describe the conflict (technical disagreement, team dynamic, stakeholder alignment issue).*

## Task
*What was the desired outcome or resolution goal?*

## Action
*How did you facilitate communication, analyze choices, or negotiate a resolution? What compromises or decisions were made?*

## Result
*What was the outcome? How did the relationship, project delivery, or team dynamic improve?*
