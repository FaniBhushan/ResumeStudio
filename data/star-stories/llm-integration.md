# STAR Story: LiquidAI LLM Integration POC

## Situation
Mercedes-Benz decided to prototype the integration of the CPU-based LiquidAI model for ASR/NLU (Automated Speech Recognition / Natural Language Understanding) detection in the US market. The project had high executive visibility but faced strict timeline constraints and local resource shortages.

## Task
My task was to lead the Dialog Engine integration (the central coordination subsystem of the onboard MBUX speech platform) for this CPU-based LLM, acting as the face of the platform for cross-functional engineering teams in both Germany and the USA.

## Action
*   **System Architecture & Integration:** Led the technical design and API alignment of the Dialog Engine's integration layer with the LiquidAI model.
*   **Cross-border Technical Face:** Coordinated requirements and resolved integration blockers between the model team in the US and the embedded platform team in Germany.
*   **Quality Assurance:** Oversaw the test drive validation, vehicle integration testing, and bug triage to ensure robust performance on CPU constraints.

## Result
*   **On-Time Delivery:** Delivered the integration prototype on schedule with zero bugs.
*   **Significant Budget Win:** The success of the POC secured final approval for production release and unlocked a **4.5 FTE team budget** during a corporate-wide budget freeze.
*   **Market Impact:** Enabled Mercedes-Benz to deploy a key voice assistant upgrade directly addressing customer experience ratings in the US market.
