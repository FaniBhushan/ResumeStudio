# STAR Story: Architecture Decisions

## Situation
*What system, scaling, or performance challenge required an architectural change or design?*

## Task
*What were the requirements, constraints, and non-functional requirements (scalability, availability, cost)?*

## Action
*What trade-offs did you evaluate? What architecture/design did you select and how did you lead the implementation?*

## Result
*What was the measurable outcome? (e.g., performance impact, cost reductions, system robustness).*
