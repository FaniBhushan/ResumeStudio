# STAR Story: Speech Assistant Product Owner (PO) Role

## Situation
I was relatively new to the Speech Assistant team at MBition when the team's Product Owner went absent. This left the team without product leadership or direction. No other senior members came forward to shoulder the responsibility during this critical time.

## Task
I needed to step up, take complete ownership of the Product Owner duties, and guide the team through a high-stakes, onsite PI (Program Increment) Planning event for the entire Speech Domain Agile Release Train (ART) in Stuttgart/Berlin. This event had high visibility, with all business owners, cross-functional leads, and key stakeholders present onsite.

## Action
*   **Rapid Upskilling:** Immediately completed my Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification to build formal product management skills.
*   **Backlog & Capacity Management:** Took charge of backlog refinement, capacity planning, and task distribution, aligning development scope with technical capacity.
*   **Onsite Stakeholder Coordination:** Acted as the primary point of contact and lead representative for the Speech Assistant domain during the onsite PI Planning session, coordinating with business owners to prioritize features and resolve dependencies.
*   **Crisis & Escalation Resolution:** Handled critical escalations during integration and maturity testing by leading bug triage and managing high-pressure situations when management-raised blocker bugs threatened the release schedule.

## Result
*   **Successful Onsite PI Planning:** Delivered a fully aligned and approved PI plan with zero planning delays or scope mismatch.
*   **Velocity Maintenance:** Maintained team delivery speed, cleared the blocker bug backlog, and met release deadlines under tight management scrutiny.
*   **Appreciation:** Received formal appreciation from domain stakeholders and my engineering lead for leadership under pressure.
