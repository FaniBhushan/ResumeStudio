# STAR Story: Stakeholder Management

## Situation
*What was the project context and who were the key stakeholders (clients, executives, product owners, cross-functional teams)?*

## Task
*What misalignment or communication challenge needed to be resolved to align expectations?*

## Action
*How did you manage expectations, negotiate scope, communicate progress, or align different teams?*

## Result
*What was the outcome? How was trust established and did the project succeed?*
